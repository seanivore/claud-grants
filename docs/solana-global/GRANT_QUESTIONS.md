# Solana Foundation Funding Application
The goal of the Solana Foundation Grants Program is to fund public goods for the Solana ecosystem. 

- Please do not include website links for questions that do not ask for them.
- All answers should be written out in the fields below unless otherwise stated. 

## The following document will walk you through what you need to do to answer each question in the application. [Pasted below](https://tinyurl.com/y2abys36)

1. **Company / Project name:** 
   - Use the name that the company / project is known by (legal name not necessary).

2. **Website URL:** 
   - Make sure the URL isn’t broken. 
   - If there is no website, include a link to relevant material if you have (examples: GitHub, YouTube demo, slide deck, etc). 
   - If you have more than one link to share, include the other links in the “Your project / idea” section. 

3. **Country:** 
   - Name the primary country of operations.

4. **First name:** 
   - This is where the applicant’s legal first name should be written. 
   - Please do not use a pseudonym.

5. **Last name:** 
   - This is where the applicant’s legal last name should be written.

6. **Email Address:** 
   - This is where the applicant’s email address that is associated with the company / project should be written.

7. **Which funding category are you applying for?** 
   - If the company / project touches multiple verticals, select the dropdown option that makes the most sense.
     - AI
     - Commerce/Loyalty
     - Community
     - Consumer
     - DeFi
     - DePIN
     - Developer Education & Ecosystem 
     - Developer Tooling
     - Games
     - Infrastructure
     - Payments
     - Research
     - Stablecoins

8. **Your project / idea** text field: 
   `What are you building? If you are applying for a Developer Tooling grant, please provide the link to your proposal created using the template above` 
   - Please include a clear overview of what your project is and why this is something that you want to work on. 
   - A deck / presentation is not necessary but would be helpful.
   - Example of a great answer: 

        > DECENTRALIZED "AMAZON" POWERED BY BLOCKCHAIN:

        > Please see our presentation that accompanies this proposal: <hyperlink>

        > We are here to help e-commerce merchants, especially small ones, to make more money. The e-commerce domain is a huge market counting up to over $1 trillion in revenue for 2022 in the US alone.

        > We are developing a marketplace platform for Solana that enables any website to be a storefront to list any merchant's product or services so that their private-domain customers can buy the merchant's products. This way the merchant gains more exposure and sales.

9. **Funding amount** text field: 
    `How much funding are you looking for? (Benchmark: our average funding size is ~$40,000 USD). Note: AI grants are currently capped at $25,000` 
    - Please be thoughtful about the funding amount that you are requesting. 
    - Funding amount should be intentional and explained in the next section.

10.  **Budget proposal** text field: 
    `We are looking for measurable milestones for both Product Development and Adoption. Please be specific.`
    - The Solana Foundation provides milestone-based funding. In this section, please write out milestones that you believe your project / company can achieve with the help of grant money. 
    - Milestones should be written to promise a payment that is contingent upon certain criteria being met. 
    - In your budget proposal, explain what each portion of your total grant will be used for and the measurable results that that money will help your project produce. Please note that the example below uses arbitrary numbers.
    - Example of a great answer: 

        > Milestone 1 - $10,000.00 to finish development of software (estimated time to complete: 1 month)

        > Complete development of website
        > Perform validation and user testing

        > Milestone 2 - $15,000.00 to launch on mainnet (estimated time to complete: 2 months)

        > Milestone 3 - $20,000.00 to onboard X # of users onto the platform (estimated time to complete: 1 month)

11.  **Relevant metrics about the usage of your project/product** text field 
    - Relevant metrics could be # of page visits, MAUs, satisfaction scores, # of sign-ups, units sold, etc.

12.  **Why You?** text field: 
   `Why are you and your team the best people to build this product? What edge do you have over the competition?`
   - The answer to this question should be at least 100 words. 
   - If you have experience building in the Solana ecosystem or have placed in any Solana hackathons, please make sure to include an overview in your answer. 
   - In addition, if you have experience building in web3 or founding a startup, include it in your answer. 
   - Lastly, please highlight any relevant developer experience that you have.

13.  **Competition** text field: 
   `Who is a direct competitor to your project?`
   - The answer to this question should be at least 100 words. 
   - Please explain the competitive advantages and differentiators that you / your project possess. 
   - In addition, list the companies / projects that you think are your competitors.

14.  **How is this project going to benefit the broader Solana community, especially other developers?** text field: 
   `Think about things beyond bringing new users to the network.`
   - The answer to this question should be at least 100 words. 
   - The Solana Foundation supports projects that generate public goods for the community and allow other ecosystem participants to learn from and build on your work. 
   - Please ensure to highlight how your project is a public good. 
   - Public goods are usually projects that are in active development and are either open source (if it’s code) or have a free community offering.
