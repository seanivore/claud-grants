### Funding Category

### Your Project / Idea 
Here's where we're golden for having been so thorough with our [GRANTME.md](/Users/seanivore/Development/claud-grants/GRANTME.md) document. 

Their example immediately makes it clear we are in the right place. I think when re-tooling the GRANTME.md document, we'll should lean into two main areas: 

1. Those `crazy numbers of potential users based on the drastic increase in the number of developers in the world` that it felt like, 'ehh, should we?' when writing the document up for the micro-grant. And now here it is like `YES, yes we should fully pull out all the stops in defining the implications of (A) that many new developers and (B) that tech startups are so much smaller.` We can play up that it will take a while for the economy to adjust, and that our project is exactly the kind of thing that will help the market shift towards there being space for more tech startups. 
   - We can dig more into the employment pipeline, and we should add information about how "Alumni" community members are just one of the many ways we'll also be able to make connections to investors. 
   - There should be classes on things like, What to ask the startup interviewing you?, and How to pitch your startup to investors?
   - We'll really lean into the fact that these will all be community created features that we'll just need to pull the strings for. That means we'll be ensuring that community members have legitimate ways to not only contribute to the project, but that by doing so they're building their resume and portfolio. 
   - Additionally, we can consider things like partnering with companies like WellFound; basically some way for us to be able to apply our built in rating systems to a database of startups, as well as whoever the big names in tech that are hiring at the time. 

2. We also will need to find a really tactful way to include the `Community owned Knowledge Graph of the first-ever robust collection of information about how developers learn and grow in the modern age, and, importantly, how Solana is a part of that`.

Okay, you might by now noticed that I'm not at all harping on, or even mentioning, the term 'AI'. I think this is an important shift in our branding strategy. We're not a 'AI' company, we're a 'developer education' company. We're not going to be building AI tools, we're going to be building tools that help developers learn and grow. 

My primary reason for feeling this makes sense is because, it is the new normal. We can't say "AI Artist" forever. We need to presume these things. Sure we'll need the term to attract users at first, but otherwise I don't think it will make sense to continue to use it. 

My secondary reason, and the entire reason that I even considered starting to think about it this way, is because of this next section. 

### Funding Amount 

- They state their average funding size is $40,000 USD 
- They mention that AI grants are currently capped at $25,000 

And so now you see why what might have seemed like a "oh, crap" moment, is actually a helpful reminder that everything we're doing is far bigger than AI. And honestly, the only role that AI plays into functionality thus far is that the tool you use to participate in the community requires an AI to use the Model Context Protocol. Which even in itself makes sense with this new way of thinking: Because an AI will always been needed for any kind of development in the future. Really, in writing this, I feel so strongly that I'd even be willing to be super firm regarding it if it was for some reason brought up. That said, I find it hard to imagine any kind of grant they might be looking at right now that wouldn't involve AI in some way. AI may have created the shift in the industry, which creates this opportunity in more ways than one, but, if it wasn't for the fact that AI is central to development, and anyone who isn't on board with that is self-sabotaging, then the entire thing would still be possible. Less opportune, but still possible.
