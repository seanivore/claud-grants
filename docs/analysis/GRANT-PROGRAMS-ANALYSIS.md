# Grant Programs Analysis

## Program Overview & Requirements

### 1. Solana Grants Program
- **Focus Areas**: 
  - Developer Tooling 
  - Education
  - DAO Tooling
  - Censorship Resistance
  - Payments/Solana Pay
  - Financial Inclusion
  - Climate Change
  - Academic Research

- **Eligibility**:
  - Individuals
  - Independent teams
  - Governments
  - Nonprofits
  - Companies
  - Universities
  - Academics

- **Application Requirements**:
  - Must create public good for Solana network
  - Milestone-based funding structure
  - Rolling basis review

### 2. Metis Development Grants
- **Grant Size**: Up to $30,000 USD
- **Focus Areas**:
  - New project deployments
  - Product development
  - Builder Mining rewards
  - Sequencer mining
  - Small audits
  - Liquidity provision

### 3. Sui Foundation Grants
- **Grant Size**: $30-40K typical, $25K for Academic Research
- **Focus Areas**:
  - Developer tools
  - Education
  - Academic research
  - Community building

### 4. Tokamak Network
- **Grant Size**: Up to $5,000 for small grants
- **Focus Areas**:
  - Open-source tooling
  - Infrastructure
  - Protocol enhancements
  - Experimental projects

### 5. SuperTeam Grants
- **Grant Size**: 
  - Microgrants: $7,500-$10,000 USDC
  - Larger grants: TBD
- **Focus Areas**:
  - Developer tools
  - Community building
  - Educational initiatives
  - Ecosystem growth
- **Application Structure**:
  - One-Line Pitch
  - Problem Statement (100 words)
  - Solution (150 words)
  - Why This Matters (100 words)
  - Detailed Deliverables with timelines
  - Clear Budget Breakdown
  - Team Experience & Links
  - Additional Notes/Commitments

### 6. BuildWithMonkeDAO Grants
- **Application**: Google Form based
- **Focus Areas**: TBD
- **Research Needed**: Processing time, grant sizes

### 7. FranklinDAO Grants
- **Platform**: SuperTeam
- **Focus Areas**: TBD
- **Research Needed**: Processing time, grant sizes

### 8. Solana Foundation Stanford Grants
- **Platform**: SuperTeam
- **Focus Areas**: Academic/Research
- **Research Needed**: Processing time, grant sizes
